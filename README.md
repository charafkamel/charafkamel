#### ✨ Highlights

- 🏆 **[Any Question Any Place](https://github.com/charafkamel/vitol)**: **won the AXA Challenge at a 2024 hackathon.** Ask a satellite image a question in plain English ("how many storage tanks wider than 5 m?") and an LLM agent picks the right computer-vision tools to answer it.
- 🧼 **[DetoxText](https://github.com/charafkamel/Deep_Learning)**: rewrites toxic sentences into neutral ones. Fine-tuned Qwen3-0.6B and T5 on ParaDetox with SFT, a custom loss, and GRPO reinforcement learning with our own reward.
- 🛢️ **[What's in a Barrel?](https://challenger-com480.vercel.app)**: a D3 scrollytelling piece that follows one barrel of crude oil from a kilometre underground to the objects in your day ([code](https://github.com/com-480-data-visualization/Challenger)).
- 🔌 **Open source:** added a Compresr context-compression guardrail to [LiteLLM](https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm/pull/33019) ([docs merged](https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm-docs/pull/657)).
